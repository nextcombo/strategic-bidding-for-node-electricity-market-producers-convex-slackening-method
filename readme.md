# Strategic Bidding for Node Electricity Market Producers: Convex Slackening Method (Matlab Implementation)

Strategic Bidding in Node Electricity Markets: Convex Slackening Method (Matlab Implementation)
The strategic bidding problem in electricity markets has been widely studied, typically solved through the formulation of complex dual-level optimization problems. The most advanced method for solving such problems is to recast them as mixed integer linear programs (MILP). However, once the network scale expands, the scope of dispatching increases, or considering randomness, the compression time of this MILP recasting sharply increases. In this paper, we take a fundamentally different approach and propose an effective and practical convex programming tool to solve the strategic bidding problem in node electricity markets for producers. Our method is inspired by Schmudgen's Positivstellensatz theorem from semi-algebraic geometry; however, we subsequently obtain near-optimal bidding solutions through several steps based on convex optimization and mixed integer programming, which are proven in several numerical case studies and have significant advantages in reducing computational time. When we increase the number of dispatching scopes or random scenarios, the computational time of the state-of-the-art MILP methods grows exponentially, whereas our method's computational time is linear.
